What it is呢個係咩

OpenShort is Dawn's Door: the top of the school's funnel. OpenShort (seconds, irresistible) → the halls (minutes, discovery) → OpenTeacher (the deep path, mastery). Nobody starts at the hardest door; everybody can start at the fun one. The feedstock already exists: every card in every hall cuts into shorts, auto-generated through the canon pipeline, labelled and source-backed. The founding baseline is binding: OpenShort must be more fun than TikTok — the hook is quality and curiosity, never dark patterns, never rage-bait, never fake claims.

OpenShort 係 Dawn 嘅「門」：學校漏斗嘅頂層。OpenShort（幾秒，抗拒唔到）→ 大廳（幾分鐘，探索）→ OpenTeacher（深路，精通）。冇人會由最難嗰道門開始；人人都可以由最好玩嗰道門開始。材料已經存在：每個大廳嘅每張卡都裁成 Shorts，經 canon pipeline 自動生成，有標籤、有來源。立派底線係硬性嘅：OpenShort 必須好玩過 TikTok——勾人嘅係質素同好奇心，永遠唔係 dark patterns、唔係 rage-bait、唔係假 claim。

END GOAL

People leave TikTok because OpenShort is more fun — learning that is good-looking and fun, delivering the world's canon into a person's daily life without effort or intention, and every short is a door into depth, not a cage.

人哋離開 TikTok，因為 OpenShort 更好玩——又靚又好玩嘅學習，毫不費力咁將世界嘅 canon 送入每個人嘅日常；每條 Short 都係通往深度嘅門，唔係困住你嘅籠。
